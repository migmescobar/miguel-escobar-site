This is the writing section of the site — a place for notes on communications, editorial
craft, content governance, and whatever in culture and tech has my attention. Think of it as
a working notebook rather than a polished magazine.

If you're reading this, it's the first, placeholder post. It exists so the writing pages have
something to render. Replace it with the real thing whenever you're ready — the layout you're
looking at now is exactly what your posts will use.

The house style is the same one I bring to everything else: honest, helpful, and human. Short
where short will do, longer when a subject earns it.

New posts appear here automatically, newest first. Each one is a single Markdown file, and any
post marked as a draft stays hidden until it's ready — so I can think out loud in private
before anything goes live.
