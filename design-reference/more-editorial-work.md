### Politics in Esquire
In my time with Esquire, I produced and published features on politicians and politically controversial figures. These include former presidential spokesperson [Salvador Panelo](https://www.esquiremag.ph/long-reads/esqa/salvador-panelo-a1513-a1521-20160919-lfrm3), former senator [Dick Gordon](https://www.esquiremag.ph/long-reads/what-ive-learned/richard-gordon-what-i-ve-learned-a00207-20170717-lfrm), and political influencer [Mocha Uson](https://www.esquiremag.ph/long-reads/profiles/mocha-uson-full-text-a1521-20161202-lfrm4).

Through this work, I learned firsthand about the roles and responsibilities of media in a democracy.

### Other Big Esquire Features
Politics and current events were just among many areas we covered at Esquire. I was also fortunate to write and produce features in style and entertainment, including [big celebrity interviews](https://www.esquiremag.ph/life/women/anne-curtis-smith-is-a-woman-we-love-a00207-20171111), [film reviews](https://www.esquiremag.ph/culture/movies-and-tv/goyo-cinematographer-pong-ignacio-frame-epic-a00203-20180910-lfrm), and [fashion editorials](https://www.esquiremag.ph/style/fashion/local-denimheads-have-produced-the-first-filipino-made-raw-selvedge-jeans-a00207-20171024-lfrm2).

### Tatler Asia's Most Influential
Asia's Most Influential is one of Tatler Asia's signature editorial products. Every year, Tatler publishes a list of profiles of over 500 influential people from various fields. In 2021 [I contributed](https://www.tatlerasia.com/power-purpose/ideas-education/art-influence-and-the-purveyors-responsibility-according-to-top-auctioneer-jonathan-crockett) over 100 of those profiles, including honourees in Singapore and the Philippines. It was, for me, a writing exercise that demanded quick writing and fast-turnaround fact-checking.

Image: editorial_03_tatler.jpg

### Smile Magazine Cover Story
Barotac Nuevo, Iloilo, is the one town in the entire Philippines that loves football more than basketball. I wrote a feature on Barotac Nuevo in 2020 for the in-flight magazine of Cebu Pacific Air. It would become the cover story for that issue.
